How to create a free AI PowerPoint: The Tutorial

Here is a simple method to generate your first presentation for free.

Step 1: Choose your AI presentation generator

There are several tools on the market. Some are complex, others act as mere "add-ons".

SlideAI stands out for its simplicity and generous trial offer. Unlike other tools that block PDF export behind an immediate paywall, SlideAI allows you to generate and export for free your first presentations via the "Money Back Guarantee" Freelance Pack (or free trial depending on periods).

Step 2: Describe your topic or import your content

This is the magic of "Prompt-to-Presentation".

Instead of creating slide by slide, you simply give a title or a document to the AI.

Sample prompt:

"Act as a marketing expert. Create a 10-slide presentation to launch a new ethical coffee brand in the US. Include market analysis, personas, and a media plan."

If you already have a document (Word, PDF), SlideAI can read it and turn it into slides. Ideal for turning a boring report into a visual presentation.

Step 3: Let the AI work (40 seconds)

Once you click "Generate", the algorithm will:

  1. Analyze your request to understand the context.
  2. Create the outline (slide titles).
  3. Write the content of each slide (impactful and concise).
  4. Generate visuals and layout.

All this is done in less than a minute.

Step 4: Customize and Export

Once the presentation is generated, you are in the editor. It's time to add your personal touch.

  • Change colors to match your brand.
  • Modify images if needed.
  • Adjust texts.

Finally, click Export. You get a high-definition PDF or editable PowerPoint (depending on the tool), ready to be presented.
