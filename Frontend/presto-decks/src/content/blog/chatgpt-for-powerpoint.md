# How to Create a PowerPoint Presentation with ChatGPT (2026 Guide)

You already have the content in ChatGPT. Now you need to turn it into a real PowerPoint presentation you can present, edit, and export.

That is where most people lose time. [ChatGPT](https://chat.openai.com/) is excellent for outlines, talking points, and draft copy, but it does not natively give you a polished [`.pptx` file](/create).

In this guide, you will see **3 ways to create a PowerPoint presentation with ChatGPT**, from manual copy-paste to a faster AI workflow built for slides.

## Method 1: Manual Copy-Paste (The Slow Method)

This is what 90% of people do:
1.  Ask ChatGPT: *"Generate an outline for a presentation on XYZ"*. (💡 Tip: Follow our guide on [The Perfect Pitch Deck Structure](/blog/perfect-pitch-deck-structure) to get the best prompt).
2.  Open PowerPoint.
3.  Create a slide for each key point.
4.  Copy the title, paste into PowerPoint.
5.  Copy the text, paste into PowerPoint.
6.  Search for images on Google, paste them...

**Estimated time:** 2 to 4 hours for 15 slides.
**Result:** Often boring and time-consuming.

## Method 2: Using VBA Code (The "Geek" Method)

A trick is to ask ChatGPT to write a **VBA macro** to [generate slides](/create).

**The prompt to use:**
> *"Act as a VBA expert. Write me a VBA macro to create a PowerPoint presentation on [TOPIC]. Create 10 slides with titles and content."*

Then, you need to:
1.  Open PowerPoint.
2.  Press ALT + F11 to open the VBA editor.
3.  Insert a module, paste the code, and run.

**Advantage:** Faster than copy-pasting.
**Disadvantage:** The slides are **white and empty**. No design, no images, just raw text. You have to design everything by hand afterwards.
